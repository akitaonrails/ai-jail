# v2.6.4

A reliability fix for filtered egress. Transient listener failures no longer
disable proxy access for the remainder of a sandbox session.

Backward compatible: every pre-2.6.4 `.ai-jail` keeps working.

## Fixed

- **Filtered egress recovers after a transient `accept()` failure (#151).**
  The supervisor's TCP and Unix proxy listeners and the in-sandbox bridge used
  iterator loops that ended permanently on the first failed accept. Descriptor
  exhaustion or an aborted client could therefore leave every later connection
  refused until the sandbox exited. All three listeners now retry after a
  bounded delay, avoiding a busy loop while preserving the session. Internal
  bridge retries also inherit `--exec` quiet mode.
