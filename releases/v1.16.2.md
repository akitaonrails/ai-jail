# v1.16.2

## Fix resolv.conf regression on NixOS and WSL (issue #93)

v1.16.1 added a second bind mount at `/etc/resolv.conf` whenever the
canonical target differed from `/etc/resolv.conf`. This broke NixOS and
Debian WSL, where `/etc/resolv.conf` is a symlink whose chain passes
through `/etc/static` or `/mnt/wsl`; bwrap could not create the mount
because intermediate directories were missing inside the sandbox.

### Fixed

- Only emit an extra resolv bind when the *first symlink hop* lives under
  `/run/` or `/tmp/` (e.g. Fedora toolbox's `/run/host/etc/resolv.conf`).
- Symlink chains whose intermediate hops live outside tmpfs (NixOS
  `/nix/store/...`, WSL `/mnt/wsl/...`) keep the previous single-canonical
  behavior and no longer get a broken second bind.

### Validation

- Added `resolve_relative_symlink`/`normalize_path` helpers and wired the
  intermediate-mount decision through `SandboxGuard`/`MountSources`.
- Updated `resolv_intermediate_tmpfs_hop_is_mounted` to assert the toolbox
  case emits both the canonical and intermediate binds.
- Kept `resolv_regular_file_does_not_double_mount` to ensure regular-file
  `/etc/resolv.conf` still produces exactly one bind.
